# Finite field dressings — 4 October 2026

> **Development-workspace record.** This describes an isolated gameplay branch.
> See [published progress](../../verification/published-progress.md) for `main`.

Select a packed **Camisa de lino** in the tactical inventory. **Preparar vendas**
consumes exactly one shirt and adds three carried dressings. The shirt must have
at least 50% condition. A worn, held or cursor garment needs ordinary packing
first. Ponchos, hats and trousers are not recipe inputs.

Preparation costs 20 AP in combat. In exploration, the shared paid-work rule
advances two seconds without subtracting AP. Preparation lowers a raised gun.
It does not restore HP, stop bleeding or change ammunition. To use the result,
prepare the dressings in the hand and perform ordinary first aid. Care retains
its existing skill, reach, AP, work and dressing-consumption rules.

The shirt's 0.6 kg becomes three 0.2 kg dressings. Its exact owned identity is
consumed once. A legacy equivalent stack loses only one member. The shared
inventory extraction/addition rules check final pocket capacity before any
payment or mutation. A full valid pack can succeed because removing a garment
frees a large pocket. The inspected selection retains its original owner, key,
record and physical pocket fingerprint; a replacement or changed shirt requires
new selection.

Fresh Retiro's actual armory chest contains one identified shirt. New hires keep
their ordinary personal clothing; preparation issues no extra shirt. Saved,
opened and depleted chests retain their contents through reentry. The recipe,
condition threshold, weights and work cost are game tuning. They are not a
historical medical method or a claim that damaged clothing provides bullet
protection. Broader crafting and explosive-trap choices remain open.

## Acceptance

A fresh native hire of soldier 112 pays the ordinary 1,050-peso weekly contract,
retaining 2,150 pesos. Real Retiro approach, discovery, opening and pickup acquire
the one shirt. Four ordinary inventory orders wear it, pack it, restore the
personal poncho and prepare dressings. They spend five seconds in total, with
two seconds for preparation. Two owned dressings become five; ten owned rounds,
health and money stay unchanged. Official saved replay, sector return and
reentry retain the consumed identity and empty shirt stock.

Separate prepared wounded-actor checks prove that preparation itself does not
treat the wound. Ordinary hand preparation and first aid then consume one of
the three dressings and stop bleeding without ordinary HP regeneration. Core
checks cover regional wear, actual bodyguard recipients, clipped lethal injury,
wound-time exclusion and atomic unpaid/stale-source refusal. Mounted component
checks exercise the real inventory callback and replacement-selection refusal.
This does not establish complete campaign or live browser acceptance.
