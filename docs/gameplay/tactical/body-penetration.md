# Single-ball body penetration

> **Development-workspace record.** This describes the current development
> checkout. It does not claim published-main acceptance or complete JA2 parity.

A discharged lead ball can injure a body, pass through it with less force, and strike a later body or obstacle along its physical path, with deterministic far-shot drop. A glancing exposed stone face can redirect that path once; see [stone ricochet](stone-ricochet.md). This applies to ordinary named shots, missed shots and location fire. It includes living soldiers, civilians and unconscious casualties. Collection and ID identify each body separately; a civilian and soldier with the same ID remain distinct.

The [released classic JA2 source mirror at 876ccf5d](https://github.com/dariusk/ja2/blob/876ccf5dfdad7e6821c5b26d6d783132ea1ab7a2/ja2/Build/Tactical/LOS.c#L2106) and [official Stracciatella source at 04869c8e](https://github.com/ja2-stracciatella/ja2-stracciatella/blob/04869c8e339ead1de9fefb25b7ad206b9b786825/src/game/Tactical/LOS.cc#L2024) establish conditional passage with reduced remaining impact. This predates v1.13. Granaderos adapts that behavior to its own force scale and period weapons; it does not import modern armour or special ammunition.

The force budget starts at the weapon's base damage. Cover and bodies spend that budget in intersection order. Successful body passage spends 15 force for a head, 30 for a torso or 23 for legs. It must leave positive force. The seeded chance is `clamp(incomingForce - 20, 0, 95)%`; failure lodges the ball at that body. For example, a clear 52-force torso hit has a 32% chance to continue with 22 force. These numbers are editable Granaderos tuning, not measured lead-ball data or a claim of classic numerical parity.

A ball uses at most one damage-variation draw. Later injuries scale with its remaining force; each typed body can receive only one injury. Existing bodyguard redirection also deduplicates the real damage recipient. The cover-damage setting cannot erase body or reflection force loss. Ground, actual floor slabs, exhausted force, world edges and the bounded flight limit still stop the ray. Its total horizontal-distance limit remains `max(aimDistance, effectiveRange * 2)`; each leg clips to the map and reflection does not renew that allowance. Beyond twice effective range, a single ball drops below its original straight slope. An aimed head shot can therefore intersect a lower body region or stop at the ground. Actual geometry, observed forecasts, bystander warnings and AI share that path; near-miss learning evaluates each exact segment before the actual terminal stop. The continuous quadratic and reference-height constant are explicit Granaderos tuning, informed by the primary-source distance-scaled drop structure.

Forecasts make no random draws. They trace observed bodies, assume eligible passage succeeds, and multiply successive passage chances to show conditional reach and reduced force at the selected target. Warnings include known bystanders beyond the target. Enemy AI avoids known friendly bodies before or after that target, including both rays of a paired pistol order. Hidden bodies cannot change public forecasts or reveal their identity, position or force debit. Actual downstream injury can still be observed if the injured person is visible.

Ignition, accuracy, one finite loaded charge, AP, wear, smoke, noise and game-clock gates retain their normal rules. Conditional passage adds seeded rolls after the original damage draw, so later seeded results can change. No ballistic fields enter saved state; the same saved state and orders replay the same injuries and equipment custody. Physical cone-load pellets share the stone-face rule. Cannon fire keeps its separate model.

The twelve focused tests in `tests/body-penetration.test.mjs` cover real named/location orders, successful and failed passage, body/cover order, force/probability accumulation, typed identities, bodyguard deduplication in both contact orders, region resistance, ground/floor stops, single/paired forecasts, a truthful chance below one percent, friendly-lane AI safety, hidden bodies and saved replay. The affected physical/ability batch passed 104/104 tests without skips. The reverse-order guard case also verifies distinct ordered injuries and no second interception cost through ordinary, presented and saved execution. Ordered shot presentation is checked separately.
