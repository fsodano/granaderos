# Respeto a civiles

Inés Aguirre is a fictional contract caregiver. Fresh default campaigns give her
the explicit `civilian_conscience` ability. Its condition is disclosed before
hiring. An older pinned package that omits it remains neutral.

A capable issued soldier can object when they directly witness their own troops
deliberately kill an explicitly authored noncombatant. The soldier must see both
the attacker and the victim before the fatal impact. An interior must also be
revealed. The actual impact must take the living victim to zero HP and retain
the existing intentional player responsibility record.

The first implementation covers direct lethal impacts. Accidental hits, militia
or enemy attacks, delayed bleeding, hidden events, prisoners, military actors
and already dead bodies do not create this objection. Being represented as an
NPC alone does not make a person an eligible noncombatant.

## Service and custody

The soldier makes one named complaint. The saved objection blocks later renewal
and hiring. The soldier completes the accepted paid term. Its expiry, wages,
health and equipment use keep their normal rules. The existing service departure
returns owned equipment through ordinary local custody. This rule adds no early
departure, refund, injury, morale loss or random draw.

An objection cannot be removed by assigning a different squad, dismissing a
rival, changing the active sector, or loading a save. Campaign checkpoints and
returns retain the receipt and its civilian death evidence. A clock-only
checkpoint cannot discard the sole pending report of the event.

## Authoring

Authors can add `civilian_conscience` to an eligible paid contract character in
the existing ability editor. Biography text and names never activate the rule.
Permanent and historical military service do not qualify.

The optional `encounter.noncombatant` flag identifies an eligible fixed,
nonrecruitable and unarmed world resident with permanent civilian presence.
The content validator and editor apply the same eligibility rules. It is an
authored classification, rather than an inference from a role name.

The consequence is a Granaderos fictional character rule. It does not claim the
exact classic JA2 conduct formula or a historical psychological measurement.
Contextual fears, wider changing opinions, early departures and recorded voices
remain separate requirements. Bounded acceptance and test results are recorded
in the [video review](../../verification/ja2-video-review-2026-10-03.md).
