# Serenidad al cuidar

Ángela Cejas is a fictional caregiver. Her profile says that caring for others
helps her recover her calm. The new default content package gives her the explicit
`care_composure` capability. Campaign authors can select this capability for
another character in the existing ability editor.

A successful first-aid stroke reduces the caregiver's existing tactical shock
by **up to 2 points**. The patient must be another living person in local reach
and direct sight. Military patients must share the caregiver's side. Any room containing a civilian
patient must also be disclosed. The stroke must consume an owned
dressing and improve HP, bleeding or bandaged wound coverage.

The normal first-aid rules still decide admission, action cost, treatment work
and medical practice. Self-care, a healthy or fully treated patient, a hidden
patient and a rejected action give no relief. Repeated effective strokes on a
critical patient each require another real dressing and the normal action cost.
Shock cannot fall below zero. Treatment does not provide extra morale, energy,
AP, supplies or random draws.

Shock already reduces firearm accuracy and interrupt initiative. The capability
uses those existing rules; it does not guarantee a hit or an interrupt. Normal
turn recovery can also reduce shock, independently of care. The two-point cap
is declared Granaderos tuning, rather than a measured historical rule or an
exact JA2 formula.

## Controls and persistence

The recruitment card, dossier and editor describe the condition and limit. An
allowed medical preview shows the caregiver's expected relief when it is
positive. A successful manual treatment can show a short temporary message with
the caregiver's name and actual relief.

The capability comes from the campaign's pinned character definition. An older
package that omits it stays neutral, including after a new deployment. An active
snapshot cannot add or remove a character capability through clock
synchronization, return or save loading.

The resulting shock uses the existing battle save field. The brief message uses
an action-local result and is not saved or recreated on loading. Campaign return
retains its existing transient-shock rules; this feature adds no strategic calm
reward or persistent care receipt.

## Verification scope

Focused checks cover finite supplies, effective and rejected strokes, self-care,
military and civilian patients, room visibility, clipped relief, authored and
older pinned content, medical previews and the existing save and clock paths.
The paid hostile-action acceptance and final short-suite results are recorded in
the [video review](../../verification/ja2-video-review-2026-10-03.md).

This implements one contextual personality effect. Contextual fears, evolving
opinions, complaints, later departures and distinct recorded voices remain open.
