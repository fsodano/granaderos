# Competing physical delivery beneficiaries

Fresh Retiro campaigns offer a fictional allocation of two usable wool ponchos. The player can give both to the sargento at Retiro for the recruits, or to the port foreman at Ensenada for the port watch. The first accepted garment fixes the recipient. This is authored period game content, not a claim about a documented historical errand.

The player gives actual owned garments through the ordinary held-item or selected-item order. The offer discloses the two destinations and the first-delivery commitment before custody changes. The selected contact keeps each garment's exact identity and condition. A refusal from the other contact takes no garment. Partial deliveries, departure, reentry and saves retain the commitment and the physical objects.

Full delivery remains pending until an adjacent, capable soldier speaks with the selected contact about the errand. The shared prerequisites and the recipient's sector must be under the required control. Confirmation completes the errand once and adds the existing eight points of civic support to the selected city, up to the existing limit. Retiro belongs to the Buenos Aires town; Ensenada is a separate town. The other town receives no reward. This can affect the ordinary militia admission threshold. It creates no militia, recruits, equipment or port agreement, and it pays no cash.

Death of the selected contact before confirmation fails the pending delivery. Delivered property stays with that person. Death of the unselected contact does not cancel the selected delivery. Before the first gift, either surviving recipient remains available. If both contacts have recorded deaths after the offer, the uncommitted errand fails. A completed outcome remains credited after later death or occupation.

An authored physical errand can define exactly two `beneficiaries`, each with a stable branch ID, an existing fixed nonrecruitable contact, its actual sector, delivery text and a bounded reward. The giver is one of the recipients. Both contacts are reserved against other errands and protected from deletion or removal from the map. Beneficiary delivery cannot also use an escort, an automatic reward or the separate cash-or-support `rewardChoice` mode.

Campaigns pin their definitions. Older pinned cash-or-support deliveries remain unchanged. Omitted legacy definitions and ordinary deliveries without a choice keep their existing completion rules. No new recipient is inferred from an old gift.

The saved quest records its `beneficiaryId`. The issued request and tactical state bind the same `questBeneficiaries` map. A new choice needs actual positive receipts at its selected contact in the correct scene. Existing choices cannot switch or disappear. Retained earlier scenes may precede a later commitment, but cannot hold gifts for the opposite branch. The ordinary save, clock, resume and return paths validate these conditions.
