# Nervios al quedar aislado

Fresh default Ángela Cejas content declares the explicit `nervous_isolation`
condition. Hiring and authoring controls disclose it. Older pinned content that
omits the ability remains neutral. Personality prose alone never activates it.

The condition applies at a real new player combat turn, after ordinary shock
recovery. A capable owned soldier with morale below 50 gains up to two shock
points if no capable friendly military companion is within four tactical tiles
on the same surface. Shock cannot exceed its existing limit of 20. Dead,
unconscious, exhausted, sleeping, routed, captive, surrendered and departed
people cannot activate the condition or relieve another soldier's isolation.
Hidden enemies and civilian NPCs do not affect it.

The actual shock uses the existing firearm-accuracy and interruption rules.
It adds no damage, injury, random draw or equipment cost. Movement, treatment,
weapons, action points and paid time retain their ordinary rules. Regrouping
stops further fear; it does not refund shock. Ordinary recovery remains active.

The first actual fear event in a deployment produces a brief named notice.
Loading or looking at the inventory does not create an event. Active tactical
and resume saves retain the actual shock and notice. A new deployment uses the
existing transient-shock rules and can report its own first event.

Outside deployment, the same explicit ability also applies at actual hourly
strategic morale transitions. Below 50 morale, an awake capable serving actor
without companionship loses up to one personal morale point. Moving actors
need a capable serving member of their exact party; stationary actors need
one in the same actual world cell. A ready assault is at its completed target.
Separate moving parties cannot support one another from a stored origin.

Each episode loses at most 20 points. Positive charges are at least 3600
seconds apart, after an initial next-hour charge. Active isolation suppresses
morale recovery from rest; medical and energy recovery continue. Real capable
regrouping clears the budget without refunding morale. Sleep, incapacity,
expiry and deployment pause charges without resetting that budget. The first
positive loss creates one named campaign-log entry. Loads create no event.
See the [strategic isolation evidence](../../verification/strategic-isolation-2026-10-05.md)
for exact saved receipts, location rules and declared acceptance limits.

The [classic Stracciatella source](https://github.com/ja2-stracciatella/ja2-stracciatella/blob/04869c8e339ead1de9fefb25b7ad206b9b786825/src/game/Tactical/Morale.cc#L154-L195)
checks low morale and distance from companions for nervous isolation. Granaderos
uses its own shock, timing, range and capability rules. These are declared game
tuning, not the exact classic morale formula or a medical measurement. This
does not implement underground claustrophobia, prolonged panic or recorded
voice lines. Enclosed-room fear is a separate explicit condition.
