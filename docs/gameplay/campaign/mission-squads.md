# Local squads at San Lorenzo

On the strategic campaign screen, the desk overview provides **Fuerza para San Lorenzo** to assemble multiple co-located squads for the tactical San Lorenzo mission. The active squad participates, and the player can explicitly select other eligible squads in San Nicolás. Nothing automatically pulls in reserves. The selection shows each squad's size and any availability problem before launch.

Up to eight squads and 48 hired soldiers can participate. San Martín remains one local mission ally. Squads in transit, unavailable soldiers, expired contracts, sleeping soldiers, other assignments, exhausted soldiers and an occupied assembly sector prevent participation. Existing single-squad orders still work.

The request retains each selected squad's identity, members and San Nicolás origin. Equipment and ammunition come from their actual owners and source sector. Powder is charged once. The existing coordinated-assault rule uses the first six participants to size the opposition; adding further squads does not scale the enemy force beyond that rule. This does not alter combat accuracy, enemy equipment or the commander's survival requirement.

All selected soldiers use the mission's existing southern approach. The normal arrival screen supports separate squad selection, placement and saved continuation. The strategic anchor remains San Nicolás. Return settlement keeps each squad separate, preserves deaths and supplies, and still makes the commander's death a terminal mission failure.

The full campaign ending remains outside this feature.
