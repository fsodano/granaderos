## Economy clarification

The later user instruction replaces strategic supplies and equipment commerce with town income and found equipment. Buenos Aires requires both Buenos Aires and Retiro. Ensenada and Santa Fe are separate towns. The three port representatives must be met physically after full local control. A friendly or direct conversation records the agreement; control alone does not activate income. Initial daily amounts are 8,000, 5,000 and 4,000 pesos respectively. They are game tuning, not historical revenue estimates.

The clock pays each active agreement once at midnight. Losing a required sector suspends payment. The agreement resumes after recapture. Older saves receive no automatic agreement or payment for past days. Saved conversation receipts, control and the last paid day prevent duplicate or fabricated payments.

Ordinary equipment shops, used shops, sales, automatic ammunition purchases, paid medical/toolkit refills, horse purchases and breeding are closed. Existing personal items, service equipment, owned horses and previously paid cargo retain their custody. A new service equipment issue includes ten cartridges once; renewal, deployment and reentry do not issue another allowance. Guns, typed cartridges, dressings and finite large-pocket toolkits can be recovered from authored caches and actual battle loot. Spent cache contents do not respawn.

Conquered arsenals provide finite artillery through an actual visit and chest opening. Buenos Aires has one bronze gun, Córdoba has two bronze guns, and Ensenada has one field gun and one swivel gun. Each piece has one loaded shot and six reserve shots. Recovery, local storage, transport and deployment preserve that piece's identity and remaining shots. These are declared gameplay inventories, not historical arsenal counts. Narrative foundry, army funding and fortification still have their authored costs; these do not purchase ordinary equipment.
