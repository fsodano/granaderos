# Classic JA2 gameplay requirements baseline

This document defines the classic *Jagged Alliance 2* gameplay requirements baseline for Granaderos. Each row records a required player choice or simulation capability, its classic source citations, and the Granaderos rule and acceptance condition.

Row codes (`T01`–`T10`, `C01`–`C12`, `V01`–`V10`, `P01`–`P06`, `I01`–`I10`, `E01`–`E05`, `A01`–`A07`, `R01`–`R08`, `S01`–`S09`, `W01`–`W10`) identify individual requirements referenced across gameplay, specification, and planning documents. The `G<n>` and `M<n>` citations follow the printed page numbers of the sources defined in [Scope and evidence](#scope-and-evidence).

## Scope and evidence

Preserve the choices that make classic *Jagged Alliance 2* work: individual soldiers, scarce time and equipment, information that must be discovered, interruptions, lasting wounds, independent squads, specialist work, consequential relationships, and an enemy campaign. Keep the Granaderos setting, characters, weapons, provinces, and historical campaign. A period substitute must preserve the associated choice where possible. Missing systems do not become out of scope because the current architecture lacks them.

G19,21,38; M35-36. | Action points scale on a 100-point adaptation according to attributes, experience, wounds, and breath. Search limits (three steps / 24 AP normally and a protected interrupt-step policy) and body-region scoring weights are Granaderos tuning; incoming exposure estimates torso chance. Specialist tools, held-gun/tool handovers, coordinated supply requests, wider cache searches, broader equipment choice, conservative care/grenade/artillery gun-handover exclusions, wider medical searches, wider repair-kit retrieval/sharing and general maintenance, donor reserve tuning, and broader pack/secondary ammunition choice remain required for full parity. |

## Completion rule

Full parity requires satisfying every requirement row through domain rules, usable interface controls, persistence, and end-to-end gameplay, or recording an explicit historical design exclusion with evidence. An isolated formula helper or visual label does not satisfy a requirement. Numerical tuning must be identified as classic-source-derived or a Granaderos adaptation.
