# AI: adjacent owned pack-gun handover

An enemy with a healthy charged main gun can give one unused loaded gun from its pack to an adjacent exhausted gunner. The decision uses the existing paid transfer, capacity and equipment plans, then the existing affordable useful-shot score. It keeps the donor's held gun. It creates no firearm, cartridge, recipient job or remote request.

The accepted predecessor is main `fe30bcb42ba3768481b171d9d5e8fe9a382c9cb1`, after the published firearm-serviceability correction. Before this increment, the deterministic seed-45 case has a donor with 4 internal AP, a charged owned 1805 and a loaded owned pack 1806. Its adjacent ally has 14 AP, an empty owned 1805 and no reserves or spare. Both AI choices return no action. The real enemy turn leaves both AP budgets and the target's 100 HP unchanged.

After this increment, the same unforced enemy turn gives once for 4 AP, equips the received gun for 6 AP and fires once for 8 AP. The donor keeps its exact primary record and charge. The recipient stores its exact displaced gun, fires the transferred charge and retains the incoming gun's identity and metadata. The recorded head hit causes 60 damage; the ordinary round's bleeding leaves the target at 36 HP. Both actors finish at 0 AP and the turn still takes six seconds. A separate legal player-side equivalent uses a torso shot and leaves 60 HP; that control proves the existing paid orders, not the enemy's chosen hit location. With 12 recipient AP the shared scorer can instead admit its cheaper six-AP shot; the default 14-AP result is not a fixed minimum for every shot.

Only a single ready, serviceable, explicitly owned packed gun qualifies. The recipient must have an exhausted or broken primary firearm, no useful owned backup or immediate melee alternative, room for the incoming and displaced records, and enough remaining AP for equip plus a useful shot. Both actors must independently see the same target. Friendly obstruction and the shared shot rules still apply. The transfer must be an adjacent `give` with two participants; there is no trip, relay or thrown gun.

The recipient must also be upcoming in the active enemy, reaction or autonomous-allied queue, in the same control group. A soldier that already passed, or did not qualify for the donor's reaction, receives no new gun under this policy. Between turns the existing roster order supplies the projection. The chooser adds no scheduling state and grants no AP.

Existing self-defense, maintenance and cartridge/dressing sharing keep priority. A local own-window sharing guard prevents a between-turn phase projection from choosing a gun ahead of an existing legal local supply demand. This guard starts no supply trip. Recipients using another active slot, holding an equipment cursor, owning usable grenades, or retaining medical skill and dressings are conservatively excluded. Soldiers within six cells of a stocked friendly emplacement on the same floor are also excluded. Their existing care, grenade, artillery and slot orders retain their AP and order. These exclusions do not claim optimal equipment policy for those modes.

One inherited limit remains: the existing cartridge-sharing branch can give ammunition to an empty zero-condition primary. That donation is finite but cannot produce a useful shot until the gun is serviceable. This increment preserves and tests that behavior; it does not claim to repair general supply-demand policy. T07 remains partial, including coordinated requests, wider searches, tool/held-gun handovers and broader equipment choice.

The 23 new regressions cover real turns, exact ownership and charge conservation, full capacity, authored double-barrel metadata and partial work, source/receiver inventory-key collision, finite typed reserves, both actors' sight, hidden-state invariance, source and recipient priorities, actual operator/approaching crew orders, deterministic competition, already-passed and unqualified recipients, a nested saved interruption, public command rejection and the ordinary recorder. Visible playback records transfer prepare/result, equip prepare/result, then one fire prepare/projectile/impact/result. Hidden gun transfers and equipment frames stay undisclosed; the visible injury carries no shooter or held-item cue. Recorder output equals the ordinary reducer result and restored continuation.
