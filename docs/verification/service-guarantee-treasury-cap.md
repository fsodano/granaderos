# Funded guarantee at the treasury limit

This correction retains the existing treasury-only economy and its 1,000,000,000-peso saved limit. It changes no stock offer, salary, guarantee amount, currency or art.

## Cause and policy

Eight paid-hire cases on main `ecfa67d885b38cfe59dc7a94688ba7b0038bd2eb` establish the boundary. A declared near-cap treasury value is set only after an actual authored hire, then admitted by normal restore. This is a financial boundary fixture, not naturally earned billion-peso income. Before the correction, healthy dismissal, automatic expiry and pending-arrival cancellation could succeed and produce cash above the saved limit. Salary-only cancellation had the same defect. Exact-cap and zero-guarantee controls remain admitted.

Manual dismissal requires room for its full health-based guarantee refund. Pending-arrival cancellation requires room for the full original salary plus funded guarantee before either payment or arrival removal. Insufficient room rejects the whole command with a Spanish reason: spend funds and retry. Existing stale-ID, travel and deployment guards remain in force.

Automatic expiry, deferred safe departure and exhausted custody return do not block service, clock or report completion. Their original refund becomes an owed terminal receipt. One collector runs at the end of each successful campaign command and pays available headroom in numeric `guarantee-N` order across older and newly settled automatic receipts. It updates headroom after each payment. A manual refund is paid in full first under its separate preflight policy. Safe living departure never refunds salary.

Spending through an admitted later command creates room for the owed amount. A failed command, preview or restore does not pay. The public treasury notice distinguishes the amount paid from the amount still due. The catalogue, personnel and rival-dismissal controls show the manual rejection reason. Cancellation controls show the combined amount.

## Saved accounting

Legacy version-1 terminal receipts were paid in full. They restore unchanged. The next admitted settlement upgrades the ledger to version 2 and records their existing refund as already credited, with their original settlement clock; no payment occurs during that upgrade.

Every version-2 terminal requires `creditedRefund`, `creditedAt` and `creditedSecond`. The outstanding amount is exactly `refund - creditedRefund`. Held receipts admit none of these fields. Cancelled and forfeited receipts must be fully credited; forfeiture has refund and credit zero. Only living departed receipts may remain owed. Original health, effective maximum health and refund are fixed at departure. Rehire, later health or later payments do not recompute them. Missing fields, extra fields, invalid credit bounds or clocks, and a bare version downgrade are rejected. This is strict saved-state validation, not a cryptographic anti-edit claim.

## Evidence and limits

The focused selection contains 54 game, saved-state and rendered-control checks. It includes manual exact-cap and over-cap decisions, combined and salary-only cancellation, partial and full later payments, failed/stale commands, version-1 compatibility, strict version-2 receipts, numeric FIFO ordering, rehire, actual transit expiry/arrival, saved tactical return and a single paid posta-step command that also ends another contract. The normal editor/launch/hire/renew/dismiss test remains included. Canonical type checking and production export pass.

The portable before and after probes retain eight original cases with source pins, exact paid salary/guarantee and restore results. Failed early test assumptions are retained as diagnostics: a spending fixture initially left a hire in its real arrival queue, a transit fixture initially assumed an extra remount payment, and a rejected-command restore comparison initially ignored the existing clearing of `lastError`. These are corrected fixture claims, not waived failures.

Independent review covers the final source, actual custody continuation, credit ordering and saved payment boundaries. Root release receipts identify final checks and normal preview controls separately. No whole campaign or broad full suite is claimed.

Other income sources that may exceed the treasury limit are a separate inherited boundary. This correction does not claim global treasury correctness. Optional salary death compensation, fee/risk choices and complete R03 parity remain open. No insurer or historical compensation institution is asserted.
