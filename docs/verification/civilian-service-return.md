# Known residents returning from military service

Runtime/test source: `7f6ce719985858d7f35cf5198ea2e74c5652037f`.

A previously encountered world resident no longer keeps a dormant civilian record
after leaving military service. Dismissal or contract expiry resumes that one
identity from its current service health, energy, bleeding, bandages and finite
personal supplies. A healed recruit does not regain its older civilian wounds;
a wounded former recruit does not regain its older healthy state. Its authored
placement remains authoritative. The active scene is not populated with another
copy: a later entry admits the returning resident.

The civilian wound clock starts from that current service condition. Subsequent
unloaded time can cause one death and its existing consequences. The transfer
does not invent damage for time spent in military service. Military wounds do
not acquire a guessed civilian attacker. Earlier actual civilian incidents remain
part of the identity's history.

Currently hired actors and active scripted mission allies retain their service
state. Dead actors remain military casualties; leaving the roster cannot produce
a second civilian body or a living world appearance. Bulletin candidates stay
outside the world-presence path. Captive custody is not simulated by this return.

Earlier saves with the known departed-service flag can resume the same current
condition once. Their original civilian record is validated before conversion;
unknown fields or a purported return from a recorded civilian death are rejected.
Repeated saves cannot grant health, provisions or another starting allocation.

## Verification

Five new simulations start with a real locally hired resident, paid movement and
an actual paid assault. A compact barrier layout isolates a real enemy pistol
shot and a finite thrown torch. The test then uses an ordinary retreat and the
real campaign save codec. It does not claim a played capital-victory route.

The checks cover:

- Dismissal before another civilian encounter, current wounds and spent torches,
  followed by an actual unloaded death at the correct minute with unknown
  military injury attribution.
- Contract expiry handing over current condition once, then subsequent civilian
  time causing death without duplicating the receipt or replenishing stock.
- An earlier departed flag retaining current service health during loading,
  strict rejection of unknown fields and refusal to revive a recorded corpse.
- Two actual hours of strategic medical care using two dressings, dismissal,
  a day out of service, a real return to the cell and paid rehire. Healed health
  and the remaining torches survive each change of role.
- A real enemy kill of a locally hired actor. Its military body remains after
  dismissal, waiting and saving; no living civilian appears in its former cell.

## Limits

This delivery resumes identities with an existing civilian record and an active
world placement. Actors known only through a scripted military role, unseen
starting wounds, general military bleeding outside deployment, civilian strategic
care, capture/rescue custody and full belongings transfer remain separate work.
The expiry test follows the current military clock; it does not establish
unloaded military wound simulation. Simulation checks do not establish live
browser performance or full-game completion.

The subsequent [hourly military-wound delivery](strategic-military-wounds.md)
adds a separately configured service clock. Its verification and remaining
fractional-time, custody and strategic-remains limits are recorded separately.
