# Share loose deployment cartridges

> Historical verification record. The
> [persistent ammunition delivery](strategic-ammunition-custody.md) replaces
> automatic return refunds with physical carried and sector stock. Its current
> checks supersede the refund expectations below; the original results remain history.

Runtime/test source: `cd31016d65c2d4bb4d69d449244a4fb129a71dba`.

The production supply selector includes loose cartridges. An exact reserve
quantity can move to an adjacent conscious companion for four sender AP or one
exploration second. Loaded charges remain in their respective guns. Reserve
mass follows the actual holder; the recipient can fire and reload normally.
Full active saves preserve both quantities. The six authored starting supply
allocations stay unchanged; cartridges still use the campaign deployment issue.

Retreat previously clipped each survivor's refund to that person's original
issue, which would lose a valid shared reserve. When the reported loaded/reserve
amounts match an actual player record in the tactical snapshot, settlement can
use the force's combined issue. The final total remains bounded by issued and
actually recovered cartridges. Dead holders and unrelated identities do not
return cartridges. A mismatching tactical report rejects. Reports without
matching tactical evidence retain the older individual ceiling.

This retains the current treasury/refund economy; it does not create physical
cartridge custody between deployments. Ground placement of cartridges remains
unavailable, and the supply panel explains that only adjacent handover is
supported. Receiving limits include the gun's loaded charges so that the later
accepted ammunition report cannot overflow its total bound.

reloads it, saves and retreats. The treasury receives exactly the remaining

Prepared settlement boundaries cover a deceased donor after distribution,
missing/mismatching tactical evidence, excessive reported quantities and unknown
identities. Tactical boundaries cover exhausted reserves, attempted movement of
a loaded charge, invalid or excessive quantities, combined receiving limit,
insufficient AP and distance. Exploration transfers reserve only in one second.
The mounted Home case selects cartridges, quantity and companion, explains the
unavailable ground action and reads the normal persisted reserves.

Typed ammunition, independent loading/custody between deployments, cartridge
ground placement, full pockets and complete campaign acceptance remain open.
