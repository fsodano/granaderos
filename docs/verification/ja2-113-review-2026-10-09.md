### 1. Use one physical sight policy on flat and elevated maps

**Observed:** On a 4×4 helper scene, a 2 m hay prop with `blocksSight:true` permits sight between standing actors on opposite sides. Add an unrelated roof at (3,3), and the same sight is blocked. The local obstacle and actors do not change.

**Granaderos evidence:** `hasLineOfSight` in [tactical.js](../../game/tactical.js), lines 521–533, uses a tile-only trace on flat maps. [sight-geometry.js](../../game/sight-geometry.js), lines 12, 81–94 and 118–126, selects physical volume checks when any upper surface exists. Flat and elevated maps therefore apply different prop/height rules.

**Engine evidence:** [LOS.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/LOS.cpp), lines 1694–1741 and 1819–1848, checks structure shape, ray height, windows and wall direction.

**Correction and acceptance:** Use consistent height-aware sight through tall props, low cover and windows. Compare standing, crouched and prone actors with and without a remote roof. Define diagonal corner contact separately: [projectile-cover.test.mjs](../../tests/projectile-cover.test.mjs), lines 80–85, deliberately permits the current flat corner sight case. A corner-policy change needs a stated rule; the tall-prop inconsistency is independently reproduced.

### 2. Make loose-blade condition affect damage and wear

**Observed:** A facón in the blade slot at 100%, 25% and 0% condition causes the same 32 damage under the same seed. All three keep their original condition after the hit.

**Granaderos evidence:** [tactical.js](../../game/tactical.js), lines 89–95, returns a static loose-blade profile. Lines 937 and 949 apply wear to fitted bayonets only.

**Engine evidence:** [Weapons.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/Weapons.cpp), lines 4032–4060, scales blade impact by condition and applies successful-hit wear. The repair-threshold change at lines 4063–4065 is a separate 1.13 extension.

**Correction and acceptance:** Share the condition rule across main-hand and blade-slot melee, charge and counterattack. Check thrown-blade condition separately rather than assuming the stab formula applies. Test a serviceable, worn and broken blade; preserve exact condition and identity through repairs, drops, custody and saves. Fitted bayonet condition already works.

### 3. Keep the accepted pickup behavior

**Observed:** An actor with 12 occupied pockets and both hands empty cannot recover an adjacent loaded pistol. Preview and actual pickup both reject with `No queda espacio para esa cantidad de objetos.` The ground object remains unchanged.

**Granaderos evidence:** Ground pickup in [tactical.js](../../game/tactical.js), lines 1405–1410, and container preview at line 1766 require pack insertion through [tactical-inventory.js](../../game/tactical-inventory.js), lines 356–385.

**Engine evidence:** [Items.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/Items.cpp), lines 7502–7528, permits weapon placement in empty hands. [Handle Items.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/Handle%20Items.cpp), lines 2365–2384, provides an item-cursor fallback when placement fails.

**User decision, 9 October:** The user stated that weapon pickup with empty hands is available and instructed us to leave our implementation as it is. The reproduction above covers the specific full-pocket fixture, not every ordinary pickup flow. It does not establish that weapons cannot be picked up with empty hands in general. No pickup change is authorized or planned. Ordinary pickup deliberately preserves the current hand, as [loot-batch.test.mjs](../../tests/loot-batch.test.mjs), line 26, records.

### 4. Add the selected 1.13 suppression rules

**Current gap:** Real near misses grant practice and presentation feedback, but no suppression AP loss, accumulated suppression shock or forced posture response. Injury shock already exists.

**Granaderos evidence:** [tactical.js](../../game/tactical.js), lines 868–884, invokes [near-miss practice](../../game/firearm-near-miss-practice.js) and [feedback](../../game/firearm-near-miss-feedback.js). Those helpers do not apply combat suppression. Injury shock appears at lines 963–973. [tactical-condition.js](../../game/tactical-condition.js), line 27, clamps carryover to nonnegative AP; [validate-battle.js](../../game/validate-battle.js), line 84, rejects negative AP.

**Engine evidence:** [LOS.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/LOS.cpp), lines 7260–7291, accumulates suppression near projectile passages. [Overhead.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/Overhead.cpp), lines 8791–8822 and 8857–9090, applies tolerance, AP and extended shock/cowering rules. The shipped [AP constants](../../engine/gamedir/Data-1.13/APBPConstants.ini), lines 378–392, permit AP debt. This needs its own saved state and turn-budget design; adding chatter is insufficient.

**Acceptance:** Use the actual projectile trajectory and stop point. Cover both sides, missed targets, bystanders, blocked bullets, pellets and explosions. Test caps, tolerance, posture, recovery and interrupted save/resume. Tune for finite musket fire, pellets and volleys. The shipped automatic-weapon AI thresholds do not fit muskets directly.

### 5. Apply persistent blast damage to structures and furniture

**Observed:** A normal grenade order detonates at (8,3), reducing an enemy from 100 to 45 HP. Barrels at (8,4) remain byte-equivalent and still block movement. This is a controlled simulation case, not a historical damage-threshold measurement.

**Granaderos evidence:** [tactical.js](../../game/tactical.js), lines 1061–1082, applies blast damage to units and NPCs. [props.js](../../game/props.js), lines 10–13, retains fixed collision footprints without a durability transition.

**Engine evidence:** [structure.cpp](../../engine/TileEngine/structure.cpp), lines 1779–1820 and 1979–1988, applies material resistance and retains partial structural damage. [Explosion Control.cpp](../../engine/TileEngine/Explosion%20Control.cpp), lines 724–779, damages, replaces or removes structures and handles contents. The anti-material bullet extension at structure lines 1877–1908 is a separate rule.

**Acceptance:** Add material-specific durability, damaged scenery and persistent debris/passages. Define container-content handling. Test partial/repeated damage, updated movement and sight, blast propagation after a breach and sector reentry. Existing manual crowbar and cannon tile breaches do not supply general grenade/furniture destruction.

### 6. Give prisoners agency and physical equipment before departure

**Current gap:** A captive becomes an NPC, needs an adjacent rescuer for release, and must leave beside that leader. Captive inventory is prohibited. Confiscated gear becomes a physical source-map cache only after successful escape settlement; recapture can restore it automatically. Rescue and joint evacuation already work.

**Granaderos evidence:** [detention.js](../../game/detention.js), lines 11 and 27; [prisoner-release.js](../../game/prisoner-release.js), line 22; [prisoner-escape.js](../../game/prisoner-escape.js), line 8; [prisoner-escape-return.js](../../game/prisoner-escape-return.js), lines 9–22; [campaign.js](../../game/campaign.js), lines 311–319.

**Engine evidence:** [Overhead.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/Overhead.cpp), line 10241 onward, returns a freed POW to a controllable squad when the freedom conditions hold. [Queen Command.cpp](../../engine/Strategic/Queen%20Command.cpp), lines 2886–2905, places captured belongings in physical hidden prison item pools at capture.

**Acceptance:** A conscious captive can gain freedom, recover or leave equipment, and reach an exit while guards remain. Save before freedom, pickup and departure. Preserve wounds, contracts, finite ammunition and repeated-capture identity. Put confiscated items in the prison at capture and debit custody once when recovered. Keep the existing rescue path available.

### 7. Add timed conflicts and directed opinion changes

**Current gap:** Static service refusals, preferred companions, loss letters and bounded shared-service cohesion exist. Rivalry tolerance and learned-like/hate counters do not. Generic shared service can grant positive cohesion even where a directed conflict should matter.

**Granaderos evidence:** [service-relationships.js](../../game/service-relationships.js), line 10 onward, reads authored definitions. [morale.js](../../game/morale.js), line 99 onward, updates neutral pair cohesion.

**Engine evidence:** [Strategic Merc Handler.cpp](../../engine/Strategic/Strategic%20Merc%20Handler.cpp), line 964 onward, handles co-location tolerance, complaints and departure; line 1131 onward handles learned opinions. The wider 1.13 dynamic-opinion event option is enabled in the shipped Data-1.13 settings and disabled in the period mod.

**Acceptance:** Hire one authored conflicting pair, advance actual shared service, and retain warnings and consequences through saves. Separation must affect further progression. Test directed opinions, familiar-companion losses, paid-service boundaries and time-compression stops. Do not replace existing conscience complaints or renewal refusals with duplicate systems.

### 8. Complete learning from productive work and travel

**Current gap:** Repair awards mechanical practice, but not dexterity practice. Teaching and militia instruction spend energy without the compared instructor learning. Strategic travel applies fatigue and horse wear without health/strength practice.

**Granaderos evidence:** [assignments.js](../../game/assignments.js), lines 159–171; [campaign.js](../../game/campaign.js), line 585 onward; [squad-travel.js](../../game/squad-travel.js), line 97 onward. General training and several tactical field events already exist.

**Engine evidence:** [Assignments.cpp](../../engine/Strategic/Assignments.cpp), line 5673 onward, awards repair dexterity/mechanics; line 7886 onward awards militia-instructor leadership/wisdom. [Strategic Movement.cpp](../../engine/Strategic/Strategic%20Movement.cpp), line 1677 onward, awards travel health and burden-related strength practice.

**Acceptance:** Credit productive completed work or actual travel, with declared period-game rates and caps. Blocked, cancelled and repeated empty actions must not farm growth. Preserve wound deficit when maximum HP increases. Treat wisdom as a separate design question where Granaderos does not expose it as a trainable subject.

### 9. Extend repair ownership and add the selected repair ceiling

**Current gap:** A mechanic can repair another soldier's complete carried-equipment queue, including clothes, but the assignment remains bound to one `repairTargetId`. Supported objects repair toward a literal 100%, with no lasting field-repair ceiling.

**Granaderos evidence:** [assignments.js](../../game/assignments.js), lines 53–57 and 159–165; [equipment-repair.js](../../game/equipment-repair.js), lines 28–29 and 93–94.

**Engine evidence:** [Assignments.cpp](../../engine/Strategic/Assignments.cpp), lines 5502 onward and 5648 onward, handles priority repair and other local owners. Lines 5170–5196 use per-item `sRepairThreshold` and qualified advanced repair. [Items.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/Items.cpp), lines 3252–3255, can lower that threshold after damage.

**Settings correction:** `ADVANCED_REPAIR` is **TRUE in Data-1.13**, line 3631. It is FALSE in Base and in the period mod, line 3518. Calling it disabled in the shipped 1.13 preset based on Base alone would be incorrect.

**Acceptance:** One finite assignment visits eligible local owners in a visible stable order. It skips absent, deployed or captured people. Field repair respects each item's saved ceiling; a qualified gunsmith/maestranza service can restore it under a stated rule. Preserve ceilings and finite materials through transfers, trade, capture and saves. Ground/armory repair is a separate scope choice.

### 10. Extend shared crossing geometry

**Current gaps:** Walls occupy whole blocked cells. No ordinary fence-hop terrain/action exists. Roof climb links do not provide fence crossing or 1.13 window vaulting. Directional wall edges were deliberately deferred earlier; this review re-assesses them without implementing them.

**Granaderos evidence:** [tactical.js](../../game/tactical.js), lines 436–447, blocks destination cells and diagonal orthogonal cells; [sight-geometry.js](../../game/sight-geometry.js), line 87, uses cell-wide wall volumes. [validate-battle.js](../../game/validate-battle.js), line 58, and [props.js](../../game/props.js), line 3, omit fences. [tactical-space.js](../../game/tactical-space.js), lines 40–45 and 83–86, supplies vertical links.

**Engine evidence:** [worlddef.cpp](../../engine/TileEngine/worlddef.cpp), lines 1427–1461, uses oriented wall crossings; lines 974–987 supply perpendicular fence hopping. [Points.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/Points.cpp), lines 168–184, supplies jump costs and 1.13 restrictions. [Soldier Control.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/Soldier%20Control.cpp), lines 9571–9612, handles window vaulting, including intact-window breakage and injury.

**Acceptance:** Build one shared barrier/crossing model for movement, sight, projectiles, doors, AI, demolition and saves. Test parallel wall travel, crossed edges, corners, legal landings, posture, burden, AP/energy and interruption. Add period timber fences and suitable windows through authored content. Treat this as an engine change; renderer adjustments alone cannot supply the rules.

### 11. Select further 1.13 medical rules explicitly

**Current gap:** HP, bleeding, energy, critical first aid, paid care and finite supplies exist. No distinct disease, surgery or damaged-attribute recovery state was found.

**Granaderos evidence:** [medical-care.js](../../game/medical-care.js), lines 136–165, and [first-aid.js](../../game/first-aid.js), lines 15–42.

**Engine evidence:** [Assignments.cpp](../../engine/Strategic/Assignments.cpp), lines 966–1027, handles configurable disease diagnosis/population treatment; lines 4456–4459 and 4565–4575 distinguish damaged-stat treatment from HP care. [Soldier Control.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/Soldier%20Control.cpp), lines 13142–13151 and 13504–13529, requires the new trait system, doctor skills and suitable supplies for surgery.

**Acceptance:** Define a supported period ruleset and equipment first. Then test diagnosis, finite treatments, separate HP/attribute recovery, progress and saves. The shipped Data-1.13 `DISEASE` flag is FALSE, so disease is an optional expansion candidate, not a default-parity defect in bandaging. Do not infer that all optional 1.13 systems are enabled by choosing 1.13 as the reference.

### 12. Offer a legal choice to follow a departed subgroup

**Current gap:** Campaign retreat settlement waits for encounter closure, then partitions survivors and squads. There is no immediate destination-view choice comparable to the JA2 exit dialog.

**Granaderos evidence:** [deployment-return.js](../../game/deployment-return.js), lines 93–112.

**Engine evidence:** [Strategic Exit GUI.cpp](../../engine/Tactical/Strategic%20Exit%20GUI.cpp), lines 784–813, offers single/all movement and destination-view options. It also restricts loading another sector during source-sector combat.

**Acceptance:** On a cleared field, split a group legally and choose which sector to view. Preserve both groups and sector inventories. Keep the source-combat restriction rather than promising unrestricted parallel tactical battles. This touches encounter ownership and belongs after the direct corrections.
