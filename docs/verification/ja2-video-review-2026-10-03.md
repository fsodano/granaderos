Small commits are delivery steps; they do not replace this objective with an animation-only or formula-only target.

Period alternatives must preserve a relevant choice without inventing historical evidence or a magical protective garment. The video's promotional, modding and preservation commentary is context, not a request to reproduce third-party websites, exploits or Arulco's story.
